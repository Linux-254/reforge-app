Slide 02 / Strategic Thesis

The Problem: Why Traditional Relapse Prevention Fails

90% of substance relapses occur within the 90-day post-acute withdrawal window due to binary tracking.

02

The Flaw in Conventional Care

Standard recovery applications treat sobriety as a punitive binary integer: a day count that resets to zero upon any slip. This creates intense abstinence violation effect (AVE), catastrophic shame, and hidden distress.

Furthermore, conventional apps isolate individuals in clinical sterile vacuums or unmoderated chat rooms without structured nervous system regulation or somatic restoration.

The ReForge Paradigm Shift

Sobriety is not merely the absence of a substance; it is the presence of an authentic, well-regulated nervous system, reliable boundaries, and whole-person reconstruction across 21 distinct dimensions of human vitality.

Engineered for High-Stress Moments

When a patient experiences cravings or high emotional dysregulation, complex menus and heavy text cause cognitive freeze. ReForge mobile implements strict cognitive load reduction:

  • One-Tap SOS Sound Reset: Instant access to binaural frequency audio and guided breath pacing to reduce heart rate variability within 90 seconds.
  • Offline-First Local Storage: Check-ins, boundaries, and emergency contact dialers work even when cellular reception is lost.
  • Sub-200ms Latency Touch Targets: Large 48px minimum touch areas prevent miss-taps during shaking or distress.

Slide 07 / Multi-Role Ecosystem

Four Integrated Portals for Coordinated Care

Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) connecting all stakeholders seamlessly.

07

1. Member Self-Directed Portal

Patient Facing

Private, judgment-free personal sanctuary. Features morning somatic pacing, evening release journaling, streak preservation, recovery boundaries, and offline sound resets. Complete cryptographic ownership of all personal entries.

Slide 08 / Technical Architecture

Enterprise Infrastructure & Zero-Trust Security

Built on PostgreSQL, Drizzle ORM, tRPC type-safety, and hardened cryptographic boundaries.

08

Defensible Security Stack

In substance recovery, privacy is a matter of physical and psychological safety. ReForge enforces zero-trust architecture at every boundary:

  • ✓ End-to-End Encryption: Sensitive emotional thoughts and journal payloads encrypted before network transmission.
  • ✓ Role-Based Row Level Security: Direct database checks ensure supporters cannot read clinical charts or member journal records.
  • ✓ Offline PWA Architecture: Service workers and indexed client storage guarantee uninterrupted support without dropped network states.
  • ✓ Skeleton State Orchestration: Zero content jumps or unstyled flashes during asynchronous data loading.
